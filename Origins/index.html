I've always been drawn to understanding how things work - not just mechanically, but experientially. Early on, I found myself questioning why systems behaved the way they did and how they could feel more intuitive, engaging, and human.

Software became the space where that curiosity could take form. It offered a way to combine structured problem-solving with creative exploration, allowing me to build systems that don't simply function, but invite interaction. The intersection of engineering and experience design continues to guide the work I create today.
